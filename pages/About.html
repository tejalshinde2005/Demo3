About Page

The Jungle Book is a collection of stories by Rudyard Kipling, first published in 1894, that follows the adventures of a human boy named Mowgli who is raised by wolves in the Indian jungle. He is protected by his friends, the bear Baloo and the panther Bagheera, and must face the threat of the tiger Shere Khan. The stories explore themes of loyalty, identity, and the "Law of the Jungle". Characters and plot Mowgli: A human child found and raised by a wolf pack. He learns the ways of the jungle from his animal friends but also grapples with his human identity. Baloo: A wise brown bear who teaches Mowgli the laws of the jungle, such as how to find food and recognize danger. Bagheera: A wise and cunning panther who helps protect Mowgli and guides him through the jungle. Shere Khan: The villainous tiger who sees Mowgli as prey and threatens him and the wolf pack. Other characters: The stories also feature other animals like the wolf pack, the Bandar-log (monkeys), and a jackal named Tabaqui, according to the 1994 film Wikipedia page. Themes Identity and belonging: The stories are a coming-of-age tale about Mowgli's struggle to understand where he fits in, as he is too wild for the human world but eventually grows apart from the wolf pack. Loyalty and family: The stories emphasize the bonds of family and friendship, particularly between Mowgli, his wolf family, Baloo, and Bagheera. Courage and survival: The characters repeatedly face danger and must use their wits and courage to survive in the wild. Adaptations The Jungle Book has been adapted into numerous films, including the popular 1967 Disney animated film, the 1994 live-action film, and the 2016 live-action remake. It has also been adapted for television, theater, and other media.
